Across the Centre Line will be a row of Buckets, one for each team.  All four riders are mounted at the Start/Finish End. On the signal to start Number One, carrying a ball will ride to the Centre Line and place the Tennis Ball into the bucket. They then continue to the Changeover End, dismount, pick up a Tennis Ball, remount and return to hand the ball to Number Two behind the Start/Finish Line. Numbers Two, Three and Four will complete the course in the same way in succession, with Number Four placing the last Tennis Ball into the bucket on their way back. When dismounted, the rider must hold the pony by the nearer rein throughout. The winning team will be the one whose Number Four is first over the Start/Finish Line with five Tennis Balls in their Bucket. Line stewards will replace balls knocked out of the circle, if safe to do so. If a ball is knocked into the field of play and cannot be retrieved safely by the Line Steward, the rider collecting that ball must pick it up and carry it over the Changeover Line before mounting and continuing the race. Ball and Bucket (Pairs) Two Tennis Balls for each team will be placed within a marked circle 3 metres behind the Changeover Line. Across the Centre Line will be a row of Buckets, one for each Pair. Both riders will be mounted behind the Start/Finish line  They then continue to the Changeover End, dismount, pick up a ball, remount and return to hand the ball to Number Two behind the Start/Finish Line. Number Two, will complete the course in the same way, dropping the last Tennis Ball into the bucket on his way back. The winning Pair will be the one whose Number Two is first over the Start/Finish Line with three Tennis Balls in the bucket.  Tennis Balls knocked out of the circle should be replaced by the Line Steward concerned.  If a ball is knocked into the field of play and cannot be retrieved safely by the Line Steward, the rider collecting that ball must pick it up and carry it over the Changeover Line before mounting and continuing the race. Ball and Cone There will be two Ball Cones for each team placed level with Poles 1 and 4.   A Tennis Ball is placed on the Ball Cone level with Pole 4.   Numbers One and Three will be mounted behind the Start/Finish Line and Numbers Two and Four behind the Changeover Line.    On the signal to start Number One, carrying a Tennis Ball, rides to the cone level with Pole 1 and places the Tennis Ball on it.   They then ride to the Ball Cone level with Pole 4, collect the Tennis Ball and cross the Changeover Line to hand it to Number Two.    Number Two rides to the Ball Cone level with Pole 4 and places the Tennis Ball on it.   They then ride to the Ball Cone level with Pole 1, collect the Tennis Ball and cross the Start/Finish Line to hand it to Number Three.  Numbers Three and Four complete the race in a similar manner.   The winning Team is the one whose Number Four is first over the Start/Finish Line carrying the Tennis Ball with other Tennis Ball on the Ball Cone level with Pole 4 In the case of cones knocked over or a ball being dropped, General Rules apply. Ball and Cone (Pairs) There will be two Ball Cones for each Pair placed level with Poles 1 and 4.
