Part A is how organisers add score cards, banners and a now-racing strip to the live video, and run them from a producer screen. Part A · Stream controls for organisers

Everything here is for organisers: admins, and the trainer who created the competition. Viewers only ever see the result on their video.

A1 · Score card after each race

Appears on its own once a race's results are saved. It is the whole heat table with the new points highlighted. The producer can fix, hide or re-time it before or while it shows.

Score card: viewer, portrait

About 4 seconds after a race's results are saved, the heat table slides over the bottom of the video. Every team is there, in running order, with the points just scored in a highlighted chip. An arrow shows who moved up or down. The thin amber line is the time left on screen.

Opens from the producer's queue, from the filming phone, or by tapping the card. It edits how the card looks, not the scores: fix a display name, hide a row (here, Pony Club Seniors B), change the time on screen, or hide the whole card. Points stay read-only.

A4 · Producer screen

One separate page that runs the overlays for every arena and camera angle. Open to organisers: admins, and the trainer who created the competition (the same people who can go live). Made for a laptop or tablet beside the arena.

Producer screen (laptop)

Each tile is a live angle with a picture-in-picture preview, so the producer sees what viewers see. The three buttons under each picture set that angle: red is on air, blue follows the automatic rule, grey is off. Take buttons at the bottom put the next thing on air; keys 1 to 3 do the same. Customise look opens the look and layout screen (A3).

Part B · Low signal

Built for a venue with one bar of signal: slow, but there. The aim is that the first thing people want arrives quickly and the app is honest about what it is doing.

B1 · Connection pill

Hidden while everything is live. Appears in the header when the connection is struggling, and tapping it retries.

Connection pill: timetable

The pill sits in the header, between the title and the menu, and only appears when something is wrong. The date line shortens to make room. The timetable underneath stays on screen, with the time of the last update beside the refresh button.

B3 · Lite mode

Switches on by itself for a slow connection or Data Saver, with a manual override in Settings. It trades pictures, video and motion for speed.
