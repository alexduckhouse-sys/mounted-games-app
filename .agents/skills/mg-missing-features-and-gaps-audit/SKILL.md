# Mounted Games App — Missing Features, Half-Wired Controls & Product Gaps Audit Skill

Use this skill for **Parent Agent #11 (`missing-features-parent`)** and for Claude/Codex cross-checks.

## Mission
Hunt across `https://mounted-games-app.vercel.app`, the documentation (`APP_DESIGN.md`, `PRODUCT_DETAILS.md`, `NEXT_STEPS.md`, `docs/specs/*.md`, `docs/designs/design-decisions.md`), and the codebase (`api/` + `web/src/`) for:
1. **Half-Wired Features:** Settings, toggles, or DTO fields that exist in the Creator Wizard, DB entity, or UI, but aren't actually enforced or displayed at the other end (or backend endpoints that exist with no UI button to reach them).
2. **Broken Promises / Spec Gaps:** Features described in `APP_DESIGN.md`, `PRODUCT_DETAILS.md`, or `NEXT_STEPS.md` that are missing or incomplete.
3. **Real-World Mounted Games Missing Features:** Practical features that an organiser, ring scorer, chief steward, trainer, rider, parent, or stream producer at a real competition will immediately look for and find missing.

---

## Multi-Agent Architecture (Parent → 3 Specialist Missing-Feature Subagents)

The **Missing Features Parent Agent** MUST spawn **3 specialist child subagents** in parallel:

### Subagent MF1 — Half-Wired Settings, Dead Buttons & Backend↔Frontend Parity Gaps
1. **Creator Wizard & Competition Settings End-to-End Trace:**
   - Trace every single field saved in the 10-step Creator Wizard (`web/src/pages/admin/creator/`) and `Operations` settings (`HasFinal`, `CarryPointsIntoFinal`, `DoublePointsInFinal`, `PlannedFinalRacesJson`, `MaxLanes`, stabling options, BACS details, custom dec form questions, age-group rules, pair-up toggles, max entries/waitlist).
   - Verify whether **every single saved setting actually changes behaviour** on the public competition pages, entry form, declaration form, draw generator, scoring screen, and standings! Flag any setting that is saved to the DB but ignored downstream (or shown in the UI but not persisted).
2. **Orphan API Endpoints vs Orphan UI Controls:**
   - Compare all `[HttpGet]`/`[HttpPost]`/`[HttpPut]`/`[HttpDelete]` endpoints in `api/Controllers/` against `web/src/` API calls. Are there built backend features with no UI to trigger them?
   - Conversely, are there UI buttons, links, or tabs that do nothing, show a placeholder, or hit a missing/wrong route?

### Subagent MF2 — Organiser, Show Secretary, Ring Scorer & Broadcast Missing Features
Think like a real Mounted Games Show Secretary, Ring Scorer, Chief Steward, and Stream Producer on competition weekend:
1. **Pre-Event & Entries Management Gaps:**
   - Can an organiser easily key in a late entry manually, move a team between sections, refund/cancel an entry, export stabling/entry CSVs, or chase unpaid BACS entries and missing declaration forms in bulk?
2. **On-the-Day Draw, Timetable & Scoring Gaps:**
   - What happens when a team scratches (withdraws) mid-competition after Heat 1? Can an organiser mark a team as Withdrawn/Scratched or HC cleanly without breaking Heat 2 lane draws?
   - Can a scorer or organiser easily handle a dead-heat (tie in a single race), a referee yellow/red card, a rider/horse substitution after declarations close, or a manual points penalty/bonus with a visible reason note?
   - Does Prize Giving ("Last to First" reveal with riders, ponies, and `Trained by`) have everything needed for the PA announcer and presentation steward?
3. **Print, Export, QR Codes & Broadcast Gaps:**
   - **End-to-End QR Code Verification (`uqr`, QR Generator, QR Scanner & Printed Sheet QRs):**
     - Find every place a QR code is generated or scanned across the app (Competition Sharing, Supporter/Parent/Trainer Invite links, Printable Noticeboard/Heat/Steward sheets, Scoring/Stream quick links, Team profiles).
     - Decode/inspect the exact URL encoded inside each QR code: verify it is an absolute valid URL (`https://mounted-games-app.vercel.app/...`, never a relative path without origin, `localhost`, or a broken route) and that opening that exact URL in a fresh mobile session lands on the intended destination without a 404 or login trap.
     - Check QR visual scannability: ensure every QR code has a proper high-contrast light background / quiet-zone margin so a phone camera can scan it even when the user's app is in a dark theme (`stable-night`, `ocean`) or when printed in black-and-white (`@media print`).
     - Check the in-app QR Scanner component: verify camera permission handling, error fallback when camera access is denied, and whether every QR code generated by the app is recognised by the in-app scanner.
   - Are any essential printable sheets missing (e.g. Arena Steward Gate List with Bibs & Tack ticks, Judge's Finish Order Card, Equipment/Prop Crew Checklist by Heat, Stabling Allocation Board, Prize Giving Read-Out Sheet)?

### Subagent MF3 — Competitor, Trainer, Rider, Parent & League Missing Features
Think like a Club Trainer, Solo Rider, Pony Club Parent, and League Fan:
1. **Entries, Stabling, Pair-Ups & Saved Combos:**
   - Can a trainer or rider save multiple **Rider + Horse combos** (e.g. the same rider with Pony A vs Pony B) and reuse them in 1 tap across future competitions without retyping pony details and tack every weekend?
   - How complete is the **Pair-Up / Looking for a Team** workflow from requesting a pair-up to accepting and forming a combined/mixed team?
2. **My Dashboard, Notifications & Following:**
   - Does the signed-in dashboard show all 3 core blocks clearly: (1) Action items ("Dec form needed", "Pair-up request waiting", "Unpaid entry"), (2) Today's upcoming heats for My Teams (with live estimated start time, session, heat, starting lane), and (3) Quick links to My Teams, My Signups, Dec Forms, and Club Members?
   - Check push/in-app notification triggers ("60 minutes until your heat", "Your heat's results are in", "Finals draw published", "Timetable delay / Redraw alert") and `.ics` calendar subscription feeds.
3. **Seasons, League Standings & Historical Records:**
   - Check the Season / League creation and points calculation workflow: can a season admin create a season, attach competitions at creation AND add more competitions later, configure points by finishing position, and handle ties?

Categorise every finding as:
- **[HALF-WIRED / BROKEN PROMISE]** — Exists in part of the code/UI/docs but isn't connected end-to-end.
- **[HIGH-VALUE MISSING FEATURE]** — Essential real-world Mounted Games workflow that users will expect.
- **[NICE-TO-HAVE ENHANCEMENT]** — Thoughtful quality-of-life feature for future iterations.

Include clickable `[File](file:///...)` links, exact user story, and what needs to be wired or built.
